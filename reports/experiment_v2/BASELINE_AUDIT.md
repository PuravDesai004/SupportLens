# V1 audit before new experiments

The original v1 artifact's vectorizer and model hashes match `reports/selection.json` and the local training-run files. Its 6,000 feature columns, 77 classifier class indices, and sorted labels match the official training labels. The original local tests passed (9 tests). No inconsistent preprocessing, wrong model, or label-order mismatch was found.

The owner's illustrative question returned `card_not_working` at 0.29633, followed by `declined_card_payment` at 0.07906 and `top_up_failed` at 0.04243; the v1 0.35 cutoff flagged it. The fitted word vectorizer recognized 14 of its 17 analyzed unigram/bigram occurrences. Phrases such as `working on`, `on specific`, and `specific locations` were absent from its vocabulary; `working`, `not working`, `card`, and `card is` contributed positively to the `card_not_working` logit. These observations describe the model, not a verified reason for a ground-truth label. The wording could refer to a card failing generally, being declined by a merchant, or location-related acceptance; no ground-truth label was supplied.

On the **original, already-used v1 validation split**, the existing model made 241 errors in 2,001 examples. The most common directional confusion was `top_up_failed` -> `top_up_reverted` (6); `pending_top_up` -> `top_up_failed` and `get_physical_card` -> `pin_blocked` each occurred 4 times. Public-data examples and additional counts are in `baseline_error_audit.json`. Several examples lack the detail needed to distinguish closely related intents, while wording variations can also challenge word-only features. This audit motivates testing character fragments but does not establish that they will improve accuracy.

The displayed example is an illustrative user-provided question, not a labeled benchmark or a tuning target. This report stores only its predicted labels and scores.
